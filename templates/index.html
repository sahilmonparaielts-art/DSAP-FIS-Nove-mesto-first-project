Interpretation

  • Cookies travel with matching HTTP requests; JavaScript-readable cookies are exposed to same-origin scripts.
  • localStorage, sessionStorage, and IndexedDB are readable by same-origin JavaScript. Avoid placing bearer session IDs or secrets there.
  • HttpOnly limits cookie reads from JavaScript, while CSP and safe rendering reduce injection risk. These are layered defenses.
  • Encryption at rest does not protect data from active code running in the unlocked application.
